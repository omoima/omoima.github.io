Mama's Boy

Ngwana wa Manene

Patience Moima.

My mother.

My first love.

The realest of the reals!!!

Rest in peace

Oh mama I love you. Thank you. Rest in eternal peace, earth has been different since you have left.

The speech I couldn't say on your unveiling...

Thank you. Ka leboga mama. Nenza. Pat. Mmathebe. Ngwana wa ko Top Magong. Wa Top 20.

We said the theme ke last dance. Banyana ba di rough dance ba kae? Then stop… ✋

That’s it. Mama ne a rata go thaba. Dilo tse shapo. Monaydo! Even in grief there are positive things to learn…

Ba bantsi bailo lebala. Mara bona, nna le Tsho re kase tswile re lebetse. Ditiro tsa lona re phela ka tsone.

Ha ke bolele thatha. Ngiz thulele. Uyazi mos. Mara ne le rata go yetsa gore ke ithoupe. Ke buwe. Ke ire ntwe e ke sa e tlwayelang. And for that ka leboga. Nou ke nale ditedu and ka bolela because of wena mama. (Mara that day go padile)

Dankie mama. Rest in peace. It’s not easy mara you taught us to live life fully. To love fully. And for that we’re forever grateful!

Miss our jokes and laughs. Real love. But I’m not sad or anything. I’m happy. You and I left each other on good terms. You’re at peace I know it. I smile because of you.

Loooso logolo ke ditshego" is a Setswana proverb that literally translates to "the greatest death is laughter"

I want to thank the Dlaminis. Mbalis's mom was always there. Even matric dance when they dropped me last minute, I made a plan. Car and date. Danko

In your last month you wanted me to pay back the loan I took from you. I paid it. And then we were chilled. Queen of money management. She taught me zaka! Faithful with little. Blessed with much.

You Left this world at a young age. But dankie for everything.

I just turned 25 and we celebrated my birthday in the hospital. Sad times man.

You taught us:

  • Stay close to GOD.
  • Always not be the focus.
  • Be humble.
  • Celebrate and help others!
  • Live a good life.
  • Be intentional.
  • You can do great things by doing the simple stuff right!
  • Excel at your craft
  • Be good at your job.
  • Give back.

Shoutout to Tshoma for stepping up. Thank the holy spirit. May is the month of Pentecost. Dankie ma!

After-thoughts

Thank you to Tshomarelo, once again for being there. That day you took over the speech.

I couldn't say anything, I tried to come up with a joke ya di rough dance but nah. Almost fainted, why? Cause I didn't know what to say. How do you close your mother's life. Eish ya...

Thanks to my girlfriend again. Thanks to my friends.

Thank you papa! I really love and appreciate you.

YOUR LEGACY WILL STAND...
